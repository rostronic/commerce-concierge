# Returns & Exchanges

## Return window
Unworn items in their original packaging may be returned within **60 days** of the
delivery date for a full refund to the original payment method.

## Worn shoes — the 30-day trial
Shoes that have been worn outdoors are still returnable within **30 days** of delivery
under our Trail Trial guarantee. Worn returns are refunded as **store credit**, not to
the original payment method. There is no cleaning requirement; send them back as they are.

## Items that cannot be returned
Final-sale items, gift cards, and used insoles or socks are not returnable at any point.
Items marked "final sale" say so on the product page and on the packing slip.

## Return shipping cost
Return shipping is **free** for orders over $75 — we email a prepaid UPS label. For
orders of $75 or less, a **$6.95** return shipping fee is deducted from the refund.

## Refund timing
Refunds post **5 to 7 business days** after the warehouse scans your return. Store credit
is issued immediately on scan.

## Exchanges
Exchanges for a different size or color of the same model ship free in both directions
regardless of order value, and are not counted against the 30-day worn-return window.
