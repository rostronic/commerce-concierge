# Shipping

## Standard shipping
Standard (UPS Ground) is **free on orders over $50**, otherwise **$5.95**. Delivery is
**3 to 6 business days** after the order ships.

## Expedited shipping
Two-Day Air is **$18.00** flat. Orders placed before **1:00pm ET** on a business day ship
the same day; later orders ship the next business day. We do not ship on weekends or
holidays.

## Order processing
Orders sit in "processing" while the warehouse picks and packs them, normally under 24
hours. You receive a tracking number by email when the label is scanned, at which point
the order moves to "in transit".

## Where we ship
We ship to all 50 US states plus Puerto Rico. We do **not** ship internationally, and we
cannot ship to PO boxes for expedited orders.

## Lost or missing packages
If tracking shows "delivered" but you do not have the package, wait **24 hours** (carriers
sometimes scan early), then contact us. Claims must be filed within **14 days** of the
delivery scan.
