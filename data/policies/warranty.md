# Warranty

## Coverage
Every pair carries a **one-year manufacturer warranty** from the delivery date covering
defects in materials and workmanship — separated soles, failed stitching, eyelets that
tear out, and midsoles that collapse under normal use.

## What is not covered
Normal outsole and tread wear, damage from abrasion or chemicals, and any damage from use
the shoe was not built for (for example, using a road runner for technical climbing) are
not covered. Cosmetic scuffing is not a defect.

## How to claim
Email photos of the defect and your order number to support. We reply within **2 business
days**. Approved claims are replaced with the same model in your size; if that model is
discontinued we issue full store credit.

## Warranty vs. return
A warranty claim is not a return: there is no 30- or 60-day limit, and you do not pay
return shipping on an approved claim.
